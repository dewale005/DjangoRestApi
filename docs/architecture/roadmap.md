# Delivery Roadmap

## Phase 1
Scope: IAM, tenancy, products, warehouses, stock ledger, basic POS checkout, PO + GRN.
Acceptance: idempotent checkout, no negative stock by default, audit logs, CI green.

## Phase 2
Scope: BOM/routing, MO lifecycle, issue/output/scrap, finance posting intents.
Acceptance: MO reconciliation and posting intents generated for every stock-impacting transaction.

## Phase 3
Scope: advanced POS, loyalty/promotions, transfers, returns/refunds, approvals.
Acceptance: end-of-day reconciliation and refund traceability to original sale.

## Phase 4
Scope: MRP, forecasting hooks, advanced finance, analytics, integrations.
Acceptance: MRP shortage runs, asynchronous projections, external webhooks signed.
